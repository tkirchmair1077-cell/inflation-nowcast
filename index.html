Datenlage und Quellen – Vorbehalte

  • Model status. US CPI nowcast: YELLOW – Auditor recheck pending, not validated for trading. PPI and PCE: status not audited (PCE: Auditor review pending). Research output, no trade authorization.
  • Seals. The CPI, PPI and PCE seals are entries in a local append-only hash chain. They detect accidental edits but are not an external timestamp and do not protect against the owner rewriting the whole chain.
  • CPI (Oct 2026). Own model in mode «research provisional workaround», not certified: weights (Dec-2025 relative importance), seasonal factors and as-of cut-off stamps are workarounds (d). Auditor cross-check on 10 Nov (d): with Sep-2026 relative importance the same chain gives headline 0.307 instead of 0.348. Nowcast frozen 10 Nov 10:11 Zurich, before the BLS release (V as ledger record); BLS first release headline +0.4, core +0.3 (V).
  • CPI y/y: n/a. BLS could not publish a 12-month change for Oct 2026 (no Oct-2025 index) (V). Our analytical y/y (d: headline 4.06, core 2.87, interpolated base) is not official and not comparable, so it is not shown. Headline band withheld (sequential 80% coverage 4/6, d); core 80% band 0.17–0.48, actual inside (d).
  • Consensus (U). CPI only: Trading Economics consensus column, a substitute – not Bloomberg/Reuters (m/m 0.3 headline, 0.2 core, fetched 9 Nov). CPI y/y consensus and all consensus history: no data. PPI and PCE: no consensus shown.
  • PPI (Oct 2026), sealed 11 Nov 09:44 Zurich, stage B1 (post-CPI). Final demand +0.446 (80% band 0.02…1.00), core FD +0.305 (−0.00…0.80), FD ex food, energy & trade +0.305 (0.11…0.68) (d). BLS release 13 Nov (V). Only the CPI predictors are point-in-time tested; EIA, Brent, jet fuel, Henry Hub and equity inputs are latest-vintage, so it is not a full real-time test. Trade services is baseline-only and the largest error source. Backtest (26 CPI-first months 2024–26, d): RMSE final demand 0.367 / core 0.309 vs random walk 0.513 / 0.416. The 80% band covered only 73% of outcomes (nominal 80%): bands under-cover. Energy blocks are a statistical bridge that extrapolates beyond the training range. No y/y model. A refresh before release would be a separate, later record.
  • PCE (Oct 2026), sealed 11 Nov 09:48 Zurich, stage B1 (post-CPI, pre-PPI). Headline +0.407 (80% band 0.321…0.492), derived y/y 3.92; core +0.398 (0.308…0.488), derived y/y 3.35 (d). Roughly 30% of the weight is not CPI-driven in B1: PPI-mapped units are AR(1) placeholders (21.5% of headline weight) and 7.8% are imputed, so B1 can differ noticeably from B2. The B2 record after the 13 Nov PPI will be a separate record and never replaces this one. The backtest uses latest-vintage CPI items and weights and BEA first releases as targets, not fully real-time; Oct/Nov 2025 are excluded. Backtest RMSE (d): B1 0.077 headline / 0.068 core (n=185); real-time BEA vintages 0.071 / 0.068 (n=154); about 0.07pp means a 0.1-rounded print is hit only about half the time. The 80% band is exploratory with no coverage guarantee (backtest coverage 0.82). y/y is derived (d) and the BEA Oct/Nov 2025 values may rest on estimates. BEA release 25 Nov (V).
  • Backtests. CPI: 30 quasi-real-time targets Jan 2024–Sep 2026 (d, not certified), release timing of weights unknown (U), Oct–Dec 2025 missing; last point is the sealed Oct 2026 nowcast. PPI chart: 34 post-CPI targets Apr 2023–Sep 2026, pseudo-real-time, actuals = BLS first prints (V). PCE: per-month backtest series not available in the public files; RMSE stated only.
  • No data: nowcast evolution/path (history not archived), CPI and PPI y/y nowcasts, PCE per-month backtest series, consensus history. The former simulated «Refresh» button was removed because it would have altered real figures.
  • Not shown on purpose: licensed third-party shadow indicators (used-car and rent data; internal research use only), internal or licensed benchmark and consensus comparisons, any broker-sourced content, and EA HICP (model status RED, not validated – this page covers the US only).
