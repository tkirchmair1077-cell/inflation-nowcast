⚠ Data and sources – caveats (FIPI-US)

FIPI-US is an internal staff construction (d), source intern. Values are from the frozen V12R H2 definition. The latest point is 2026-10-30. Zones are ±0.5. Not validated for an investment decision.

Momentum uses the v1.1 rule GAP3 (distance to the 3-month average) and is classified as rising, bottoming, topping or falling. This is a descriptive classification (d), not a forecast signal. The 2020-01 to 2026-10 holdout test was not statistically significant (internal FIPI-Momentum v1.1, Auditor green, 22 Nov 2026). Inflation used in that study is the current vintage only (no real-time ALFRED vintages).

The production FIPI-US V12R series starts in August 2015, the first month the V12R fixed-baseline composite is available. Before Aug 2015 (EA: Dec 2012) the line is a different basket (L9 / EXK9), spliced without adjustment; the splice decision uses the 2015–2026 (EA: 2012–2026) overlap (ex post). The history is pseudo-real-time, not a real-time record. Join gap +0.216 is close to the 0.25 threshold. Composite built by us from public and licensed survey inputs; components not shown.

Headline and core PCE on the chart are BEA chain-type price indexes via FRED (PCEPI, PCEPILFE; public domain, citation requested; index 2017=100). The 12-month change is computed here from the current vintage (d), not from real-time ALFRED vintages. PCE is a later release than the FIPI-US info date, so the last inflation month can lag the last FIPI point.

⚠ Data and sources – caveats (inflation breadth)

This is a research prototype. It is not sealed and is not a trade input. Official indexes and weights are V. Shares, diffusion and z-scores are authors' calculations (d). Judgement is marked (j). Unverified items are U. Missing official values are n/a. Nothing here is a recommendation.

Thresholds are strict greater-than / less-than. Weights are renormalised each period over items with a published rate and a strictly positive weight. The z-score is the latest diffusion minus the 2011–2019 mean, divided by the 2011–2019 standard deviation (d).

US series notes: official NSA 12-month rates for October 2025 and October 2026 are official gaps and are not plotted. The series starts in January 2011 because earlier official relative-importance vintages are n/a. About 174–179 of 182 leaves are published in a typical month (latest: 178 / 182).

The comparison panel on this desk (Australia, New Zealand, Canada) uses official quarterly (AU, NZ) or monthly (CA) CPI series; see comparison_caption.md and credit.md for those sources and the country-specific caveats.
