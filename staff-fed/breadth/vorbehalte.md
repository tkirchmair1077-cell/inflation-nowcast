# Datenlage und Quellen – Vorbehalte

This is a research prototype. It is not sealed and is not a trade input. Official indexes and weights are **V**. Shares, diffusion and z-scores are authors' calculations **(d)**. Judgement is marked **(j)**. Unverified items are **U**. Missing official values are **n/a**. Nothing here is a recommendation.

Thresholds are strict greater-than / less-than. Weights are renormalised each period over items with a published rate and a strictly positive weight. The z-score is the latest diffusion minus the 2011–2019 mean, divided by the 2011–2019 standard deviation (d).

US series notes: official NSA 12-month rates for October 2025 and October 2026 are official gaps and are not plotted. The series starts in January 2011 because earlier official relative-importance vintages are n/a. About 174–179 of 182 leaves are published in a typical month (latest: 178 / 182).

The comparison panel on this desk (Australia, New Zealand, Canada) uses official quarterly (AU, NZ) or monthly (CA) CPI series; see `comparison_caption.md` and `credit.md` for those sources and the country-specific caveats.

Licence: BLS public-domain status was not verified from a licence file in the Research Manager snapshots (U). Credits for Australia, New Zealand and Canada were verified against the licence files in those snapshots.

# Datenlage und Quellen – Vorbehalte

This is a research prototype. It is not sealed and is not a trade input. Official indexes and weights are **V**. Shares, diffusion and z-scores are authors' calculations **(d)**. Judgement is marked **(j)**. Unverified items are **U**. Missing official values are **n/a**. Nothing here is a recommendation.

Thresholds are strict greater-than / less-than. Weights are renormalised each period over items with a published rate and a strictly positive weight. The z-score is the latest diffusion minus the 2011–2019 mean, divided by the 2011–2019 standard deviation (d).

Australia: official quarterly expenditure-class CPI, eight-capital-city average, latest 2026 Q3, 87 / 87 classes. Pre-2011 uses only name-stable classes; no invented concordance.

New Zealand: official 107-leaf quarterly CPI, latest 2026 Q3, 107 / 107. Class-level weights start with the June 2011 review and are held between reviews.

Canada: 211 official published basic-aggregate leaves, not the 220-count basket total. The 9-leaf / 0.50 pp residual (2025) is not allocated. Latest month September 2026, 204 / 211 used. Years before 2023 cover only items whose published names have not changed.

Licences for the comparison panel were verified against the snapshot licence files (ABS CC BY 4.0, Stats NZ CC BY 4.0, Statistics Canada Open Licence). Credit as in `credit.md`.
