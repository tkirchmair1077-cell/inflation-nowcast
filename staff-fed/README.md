# Fed Staff Desk public page

Publish set for GitHub Pages (`inflation-nowcast/`).

- `index.html` — page (HTML + CSS + vanilla JS). Embeds a fallback copy of `data/desk.json` and, when served over HTTP, tries to fetch the JSON.
- `data/desk.json` — figures and text from the desk’s saved notes. Do not invent numbers.
- `data/pressure_gauge.json` — underlying inflation-pressure gauge (staff construction, d) at the top of Current state, next to the nowcast KPIs. Equal-weight mean of z-scores versus 2000–2019; each z is capped at ±3; the dial maps −3 to +3. Thirteen components. The four official core measures (Cleveland median CPI, Cleveland 16% trimmed-mean CPI, Dallas trimmed-mean PCE, Atlanta sticky-price core CPI) use the year-over-year (12-month) series only; monthly annualized values in the same tidy files are not in the gauge and are not on those charts. Component names are in-page links to the matching chart, or to the nearest relevant section when a series has no standalone chart (core PCE → Leading indicators → Inflation; core CPI → the CPI nowcast-versus-actual backtest). Credit lines are the tidy `credit` text on each y/y row. Cleveland y/y charts: “Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; derived chart shared under the same licence.” Dallas is labelled one month behind (latest is Sep). Thomas cleared these four series for the public page with credit lines on 13 Nov 2026, 07:11 Europe/Zurich. Built from Research Manager tidy; as of 12 Nov 2026 on this vintage (latest daily 5-year breakeven).
- The Current state section also carries the 13 Nov 2026 release-day morning note on October PPI (14:30 CET), immediately after the nowcast KPIs and alongside the gauge, before the state card. The public consensus (+0.4% / +0.3% m/m and 6.6% y/y) is shown with its Dow Jones / Stock Titan credit, which Thomas allowed on this page.
- `charts/` — US CPI nowcast-versus-actual backtest, drawn from the already-produced 6 Nov 2026 inflation-nowcast model backtest (no new model is estimated); 15 leading-indicator charts (inflation, labour, growth) from the Fed Staff Desk note of 12 Nov 2026, 17:24 Europe/Zurich; and seven 10-year Treasury composition charts refreshed 13 Nov 2026, 06:47 Europe/Zurich (H.15 and ACM through 10 Nov; Kim-Wright through 6 Nov). The publish set does not include any file with INTERNAL in its name. This set also adds four year-over-year core-inflation charts (Cleveland median CPI, Cleveland 16% trimmed-mean CPI, Dallas trimmed-mean PCE, Atlanta sticky-price core CPI), each with the tidy credit line; Cleveland charts are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The Current state section also carries the underlying inflation-pressure gauge (component names link to that indicator’s place further down the page) and, on a release day, the morning note under the nowcast KPIs, alongside the gauge.

Page section order: 1 Current state; 2 Staff view; 3 Leading indicators (three blocks: Inflation, Labour, Growth, each with a staff read versus the 6 Nov baseline); 4 10Y Treasury: where the move comes from (Split A market, Split B ACM and Kim-Wright); 5 Nowcasts; 6 Baseline; 7 Key data since last update; 8 Upcoming calendar; 9 Charts (CPI backtest).

Atlanta Fed BIE, the Richmond Fed survey price series (as a staff chart, including an equal-weight staff construction (d) of prices received, not an official index) and the Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland 1-year expected inflation series (noncommercial, DOI https://doi.org/10.26509/frbc-infexp) are on this public page with credit lines, following Thomas’s 12 Nov 13:52 decision. Cleveland Fed median and 16% trimmed-mean CPI, Dallas Fed trimmed-mean PCE and Atlanta Fed sticky-price core CPI were cleared for this page with credit lines on 13 Nov 2026, 07:11 Europe/Zurich; Cleveland year-over-year charts are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and are shared under the same licence. Every leading-indicator chart carries a source credit and a “Shown without endorsement by …” line.

The 10-year composition uses a staff breakeven (d) equal to DGS10 minus DFII10 on the same H.15 vintage (FRED T10YIE is not used), NY Fed ACM with the product URL and official-estimates disclaimer, and Board Kim-Wright (feds200533) with the staff-product notice. The 1-day Kim-Wright waterfall panel is flagged STALE. A model-based 10-year expected-inflation companion is held off this public page under the house rule and is not in this publish set.

Omitted from this public page: third-party inflation-nowcast model readings; secondary unofficial PCE estimates; licensed bank, broker and private-survey datasets. Survey-consensus figures are omitted except on the 13 Nov 2026 PPI release-day morning note, where the public consensus compiled by Dow Jones, as reported by Stock Titan, is shown with credit (Thomas allowed this figure and credit on the page). The leading-indicator labour charts include the advance initial claims for the week ended 7 Nov (revises 19 Nov). The core PCE baseline awaits PPI on 13 Nov before any reset.

Quality labels: **V** verified, **U** unverified, **(d)** derived, **own estimate** = staff judgment.

This page is a staff-style research compilation, not official central-bank staff work. Not investment advice. After editing the JSON, run `python3 ../_build/build.py` and `python3 ../_build/leak_check.py`.
