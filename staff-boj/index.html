Inflation breadth

How widespread inflation is across the basket · authors’ calculations (d) from official data

Weighted share of official CPI components whose 12-month rate is above or below fixed thresholds (strict > 2% / < 0%, 3-month trailing average), and the diffusion between the two. Shares, diffusion and z-scores are authors’ calculations (d) from official indexes and basket weights V. Research prototype, not sealed and not a forecast.

Method (d): Weighted share of official mixed leaves (finest minor groups plus mid-class leaves with no minor-group child), using official NSA year-on-year rates and official basket weights. Monthly shares are averaged over three months. Thresholds are greater than 2% and less than 0%.

Shares, diffusion and the z-score are authors' calculations (d) from official linked NSA indexes and official basket weights (V), renormalised each period over items with a published rate and a strictly positive weight. Thresholds are strict (> / <). This is a research prototype, not a sealed nowcast and not a trade input.

⚠ Datenlage und Quellen – Vorbehalte (inflation breadth)

This is a research prototype. It is not sealed and is not a trade input. Official indexes and weights are V. Shares, diffusion and z-scores are authors' calculations (d). Judgement is marked (j). Unverified items are U. Missing official values are n/a. Nothing here is a recommendation.

Thresholds are strict greater-than / less-than. Weights are renormalised each period over items with a published rate and a strictly positive weight. The z-score is the latest diffusion minus the 2011–2019 mean, divided by the 2011–2019 standard deviation (d).

Japan series notes: thresholds are strict greater than 2% and less than 0%. The plotted shares are a 3-month trailing average of monthly shares. The partition is 104 mixed leaves (finest minor groups plus mid-class leaves with no minor-group child); latest n used / n target is 104 / 104. Official 2005 weights are n/a; 2010 weights are held through December 2010 (j). No concordance between classification vintages was invented. Fresh food and energy are included; memorandum items are excluded.
